Your movies. Any screen.
A plain hard drive.

Plug in your drive, double-click: your collection becomes a beautiful library with posters, synopses and subtitles — on any Windows, Mac or Linux computer.

No subscription. No account. No server to set up. No internet once installed. Your files stay with you, on your drive — the library travels with them.

Demo captions are in French — the app itself is fully bilingual (English/French).

Try the free beta

⬇ Windows ⬇ macOS ⬇ Linux

Unzip at the root of your movie hard drive, next to your movie folders (e.g. [Movies] and [Shows] — any name containing “movies”/“films” or “series”/“shows” works), then double-click. You'll need a free TMDB key (2 min, guided in the app) and VLC for playback. Open source on GitHub.

How it works

1️⃣

Copy your movies

Drop your video files on the drive — even messy, even badly named.

2️⃣

Double-click

The app (sitting on the drive) recognizes your movies and fetches posters, synopses, dates, genres.

3️⃣

Watch anywhere

Plug the drive into any computer: your library opens, ready to play.
